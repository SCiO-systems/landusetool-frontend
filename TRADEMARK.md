## Summary

The LUP4LDN source code is free and open source under **GPL-3.0**. The **name**
"LUP4LDN" and the **LUP4LDN logo** are separate from the code and are **not**
covered by that licence. This document explains how you may — and may not — use
them.

## Code licence vs. name and logo

The GPL-3.0 grants rights over the **software code**. Consistent with GPL-3.0
§7(e), it does **not** grant any right to use the trademarks, service marks, name
or logo of the project or its maintainer. Using the code under the GPL does not
give you any right to use the LUP4LDN name or logo.

## What you may do

- Use, study, modify and redistribute the source code under the terms of GPL-3.0.
- State factually, in plain text, that your product "is based on" or "is derived
  from LUP4LDN by SCiO" — as an accurate attribution of origin, not as a product
  name or as branding.

## What you may not do without SCiO's prior written permission

- Use "LUP4LDN", or any confusingly similar name (for example "LUP4LDN 2.0",
  "newLUP4LDN", "LUP4LDN Plus"), as the name of a fork, derivative or separately
  governed tool.
- Use the LUP4LDN logo, or a confusingly similar logo, for any tool, service,
  publication or promotional material.
- Use the name or logo in any way that suggests your version is the original
  LUP4LDN, or that it is endorsed by, affiliated with, or maintained by SCiO.

## If you fork

Give your fork a clearly different name and its own logo. You may include a factual
attribution line such as: "Based on LUP4LDN, developed and maintained by SCiO P.C."
